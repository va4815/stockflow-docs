# Refactor Architecture from multi-tenant to single merchant

| Status | Created Date | Decision Date | Updated Date |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Proposed | 1 Nov 2026 |  | 1 Nov 2026 |

## Reasons

- Reduce the project complexity
- Increase the deliverable with solo developer
- Decouple different architecture experiments to other projects

## Comparison

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `Multi-tenant Architecture` | - Serve multiple merchants <br /> - Share infrastructure and reduce the cost <br /> - Centralised deployment and maintenance | - More complex to handle authorisation and data isolation <br /> More complex for data migration and database queries <br /> Higher development nad testing overhead |
| `Single Merchant Architecture` | - Simpler architecture and business logic <br /> - Stronger data isolation between merchants <br /> - Easier to debugand troubleshooting | - Higher infrastructure costs <br /> - More difficult to manage different version and deployment
